# BAO-accel

Notebooks reproducing the results of: 
L. Herold, G. E. Addison, A. G. Riess, 
*Model-independent and reconstruction-free evidence for cosmic acceleration from DESI baryon acoustic oscillations*
([arXiv:2610.40785](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40785)).

The statistics $S_F$, $S_V$ and $S_{VH}$ test for acceleration ($q<0$) directly from the DESI DR2 BAO measurements of $F_{\rm AP}$, $D_V/r_d$ and $D_H/r_d$, without assuming a cosmological model, a value of $r_d$ or $H_0$, or reconstructing $q(z)$. $S>0$ is evidence for acceleration.

| Notebook | Content |
|---|---|
| `BAO-accel.ipynb` | Flat universe: Figs. 1–4, the redshift-of-maximum figure (Appendix B), and the tables of mock and DESI DR2 results |
| `BAO-accel_curvature.ipynb` | Non-flat universe (Appendix A): statistics as a function of $\Omega_k$ and the curvature table |

The DESI DR2 BAO measurements (Table IV of [arXiv:2503.14738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14738)) are typed in directly, so no data files need to be downloaded.
